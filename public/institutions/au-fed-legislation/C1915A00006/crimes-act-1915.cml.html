1 Section 1#

(1.) This Act may be cited as the Crimes Act 1915. (2.) The Crimes Act 1914, as amended by this Act, may be cited as the Crimes Act 1914-1915. (3.) This Act shall continue in force during the continuance of the war and for six months thereafter, and no longer. Short title, citation, and duration.
