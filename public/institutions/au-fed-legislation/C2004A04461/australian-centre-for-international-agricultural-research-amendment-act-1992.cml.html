3 Section 3#

Section 3 of the Principal Act is amended by inserting the following definition: " 'international agricultural research centre' means an independent, non-profit organisation that: (a) is internationally funded; and (b) carries out: (i) research into sustainable improvements in the productivity of agriculture, forestry and fisheries in developing countries; and (ii) activities related to such research;".
