1 Subsections 6(2A) and (2B)#

Repeal the subsections, substitute: Communications to or from emergency service facilities (2A) In this section, emergency service facility means premises that are declared by the Minister, by written instrument, to be an emergency service facility. (2B) The Minister may declare premises to be an emergency service facility if the Minister is satisfied that the premises are operated by: (a) a police force or service of the Commonwealth, of a State or of the Australian Capital Territory; or (b) a fire service of a State or of the Australian Capital Territory; or (c) an ambulance service of a State or of the Australian Capital Territory; or (d) a service for despatching, or referring matters for the attention of, a force or service referred to in paragraph (a), (b) or (c); to enable that force or service, or another force or service, to deal with a request for assistance in an emergency. (2C) A declaration by the Minister under subsection (2B) is not a legislative instrument for the purposes of the Legislative Instruments Act 2003. (2D) If the Minister makes a declaration under subsection (2B), the Minister must, by legislative instrument, specify: (a) the name of the force or service operating the premises to which the declaration relates; and (b) the geographical region in which those premises are located.
