11 Cessation of staff-elected Director and deputy#

Schedule 1 Amendments (1) Any person who is the staff-elected Director immediately before the commencement of item 2 of this Schedule ceases, by force of this subitem, to be a member of the Board on the commencement of that item. (2) Any person who is the deputy of the staff-elected Director immediately before the commencement of item 5 of this Schedule ceases, by force of this subitem, to be the deputy of the staff-elected Director on the commencement of that item. [Minister’s second reading speech made in— Senate on 29 March 2006 House of Representatives on 24 May 2006] (38/06)
