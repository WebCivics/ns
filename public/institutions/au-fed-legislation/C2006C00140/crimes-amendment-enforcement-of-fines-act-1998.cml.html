Schedule 1 — Amendment of the Crimes Act

1 Subsection 3B(1)#

Repeal the subsection, substitute: (1) The Governor-General may make arrangements with the Governor of a State, the Government of the Australian Capital Territory, the Administrator of the Northern Territory or the Administrator of Norfolk Island for: (a) officers of the State or Territory to exercise powers and perform functions; and (b) facilities and procedures of the State or Territory to be made available; in relation to the carrying out or enforcement under this Act of orders made under this Act or another Act.
