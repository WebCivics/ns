1 After subsection 48(4) of Schedule 2#

Insert: (4A) Subsection (1) does not apply if: (a) the representation is in a class of representations prescribed by the regulations; and (b) the conditions (if any) prescribed by the regulations in relation to representations in that class have been complied with. Note: If the representation is in a class prescribed for paragraph (a) of this subsection and subsection (1) is complied with in relation to the representation, there is no need to also comply with any conditions prescribed for paragraph (b) of this subsection. [Minister’s second reading speech made in— House of Representatives on 29 May 2013 Senate on 17 June 2013] (141/13) ComLaw Authoritative Act C2013A00104
