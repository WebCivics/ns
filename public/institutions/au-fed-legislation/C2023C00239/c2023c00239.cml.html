15B Meaning of mortgage broker#

(1) A licensee is a mortgage broker if: (a) the licensee carries on a business of providing credit assistance in relation to credit contracts secured by mortgages over residential property; and (b) the licensee does not perform the obligations, or exercise the rights, of a credit provider in relation to the majority of those credit contracts; and (c) in carrying on the business, the licensee provides credit assistance in relation to credit contracts offered by more than one credit provider. (2) A credit representative of a licensee is a mortgage broker if: (a) the credit representative carries on a business of providing credit assistance in relation to credit contracts secured by mortgages over residential property; and (b) neither the credit representative nor the licensee performs the obligations, or exercises the rights, of a credit provider in relation to the majority of those credit contracts; and (c) in carrying on the business, the credit representative provides credit assistance in relation to credit contracts offered by more than one credit provider.

Part 3 — 5A—Mortgage brokers and mortgage

Division 1 — Introduction

158K Guide to this Part#

158N Conflicted remuneration#
